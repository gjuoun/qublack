Qu Black Mono is a monospaced typeface built for code on a strict 2:1 grid: every hanzi occupies exactly two ASCII cells, so Chinese and Latin text align in terminals, tables and source listings. The hanzi come from the Source Han Sans design (instanced from the Noto Sans SC variable weight axis, so Regular and Bold share one point structure) and the Latin from Recursive Linear, with a harmonised set of the marks that sit against hanzi.

Qu Black Mono ships four styles — Regular, Italic, Bold and Bold Italic — and covers GF Latin Core plus simplified, traditional, Hong Kong, Japanese and Korean hanzi. To contribute, see github.com/gjuoun/qublack.
