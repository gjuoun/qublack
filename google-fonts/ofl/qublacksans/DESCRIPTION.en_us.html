QuBlack Sans is the proportional sibling of QuBlack Mono: the same Source Han Sans hanzi (instanced from the Noto Sans SC variable weight axis) and Recursive Linear Latin, drawn for documents, interfaces and mixed Chinese/Latin prose rather than for the fixed 2:1 code grid.

QuBlack Sans ships four styles — Regular, Italic, Bold and Bold Italic — and covers GF Latin Core plus simplified, traditional, Hong Kong, Japanese and Korean hanzi. To contribute, see github.com/gjuoun/qublack.
